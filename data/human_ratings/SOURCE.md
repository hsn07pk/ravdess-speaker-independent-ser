# Listener ratings

Supporting information of Livingstone and Russo (2018), The Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song (RAVDESS), PLoS ONE 13(5): e0196391, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0196391, licensed CC BY 4.0. Downloaded 5.10.2026 from the PLOS corpus storage; only the file names were changed.

- `s004.xlsx` = pone.0196391.s004.xlsx, S1 Table: ratings of every file (PropCorr = share of the 10 raters who chose the intended emotion; UHR = unbiased hit rate). `src/ser/meta.py` reads the Speech sheet, rows with modality AO (file names 03-01-*).
- `s005.xlsx` = pone.0196391.s005.xlsx, S2 Table: summaries per actor.
- `s006.xlsx` = pone.0196391.s006.xlsx, S3 Table: rater confusion matrices (neutral and calm responses merged).
- `s007.xlsx` = pone.0196391.s007.xlsx, S4 Table: test-retest categories.
